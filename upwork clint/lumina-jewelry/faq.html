Orders & payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Shop Pay. Klarna is available on orders between $100 and $3,000, split into four interest-free payments.

Every payment is processed over an encrypted connection by our payment provider — we never see or store your full card number.

You can edit or cancel yourself within two hours of ordering, using the link in your confirmation email. After that, email care@luminajewelry.com and we will stop the order if it has not yet left the bench.

Engraved and made-to-order pieces cannot be changed once cutting has begun.

Every order arrives in a hand-tied Lumina box as standard — there is no gift wrap surcharge. Add a handwritten note at checkout free of charge, and we will leave the prices off the packing slip automatically for any order marked as a gift.

Yes. Klarna splits any order between $100 and $3,000 into four interest-free payments taken every two weeks, with no effect on your credit score. Choose it at checkout.

Shipping & delivery

US orders over $250 ship free by express courier in 2–3 business days. Orders under $250 ship at a flat $12. Overnight delivery is $28 if you order before 1pm ET.

Made-to-order and engraved pieces take 5–7 business days at the bench before they are dispatched — the estimate on the product page is always current.

Yes, to 40 countries at a flat $25, arriving in 5–10 business days.

For the UK, EU, Canada and Australia we calculate duties and taxes at checkout so there is nothing to pay on delivery. Everywhere else, local duties are collected by the carrier before they hand the parcel over.

Every parcel is fully insured to your door at our expense. Orders over $1,000 require an adult signature on delivery, and we cannot ship those to a locker or pickup point.

You will get a tracking link by email the moment your parcel is collected, usually late afternoon ET. The same link lives in your original confirmation email, so you never need an account to check on an order.

Returns & exchanges

Return anything unworn within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, in its original packaging and with the certificate. US returns include a free prepaid label — request it from the link in your confirmation email.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within five business days of the piece reaching us.

Engraved pieces, bespoke commissions, rings resized beyond our standard range, and gift cards are final sale. For hygiene reasons we cannot accept earrings back unless they arrived faulty.

Yes — one free exchange on any unworn piece within 30 days, including the return shipping both ways within the US.

For rings, the first resize within twelve months of purchase is free regardless of when you ask, so an exchange is often unnecessary.

Email photographs to care@luminajewelry.com within 48 hours of delivery. We will send a prepaid label and either repair or replace the piece at our cost, whichever you prefer.

Sizing & fit

We post a free sizing kit anywhere in the US — request one from any ring product page and it arrives in two days.

You can also measure the internal diameter of a ring you already wear: 15.7mm is a US 5, 16.5mm a 6, 17.3mm a 7, 18.1mm an 8. Sizes 5–9 are stocked and 3–12 are made to order.

One note: our comfort-fit interiors run very slightly small, so if you fall between two sizes, take the larger one.

A 16 inch chain sits at the collarbone, 18 inches falls just below it, and 20 inches sits mid-chest. Most of our customers choose 18 inches.

If you plan to layer two necklaces, pick lengths at least two inches apart so the pendants do not collide.

Yes. The first resize within twelve months of purchase is free, up to two sizes either way, and takes about a week at the bench. After that it is $45.

Full-eternity bands cannot be resized because the stones run the whole way around — please order those carefully, and use the free sizing kit first.

Our chain bracelets are adjustable through at least one inch, so they suit most wrists. Bangles and cuffs have a fixed inner diameter — measure your wrist with a strip of paper and compare it to the measurement on the product page before ordering.

Care & warranty

Jewelry on last, off first. Clean with warm water, a drop of dish soap and a soft brush, then dry completely before storing.

Keep pieces away from chlorine, perfume, hairspray and acid or retinol skincare. Store flat in the pouch provided rather than loose in a dish, where chains tangle and stones scratch each other.

Ours is 2.5 microns of 18k gold over solid sterling silver — ten times the legal minimum for vermeil. Worn with reasonable care it lasts for years rather than months.

If a vermeil piece does dull within the first two years, send it back and we will re-plate it free of charge.

Covered for as long as you own the piece: manufacturing defects, prong re-tipping, clasp replacement, re-stringing and re-polishing — all free.

Not covered: loss, theft and accidental damage. We can almost always repair accidental damage, and we will quote you at cost rather than at retail.

Signets, lockets and plain bands can be hand-engraved with up to twenty characters, free on signets and $35 elsewhere. Add 3–5 business days to the dispatch estimate.

Engraved pieces are final sale, so please check the spelling on the proof we email you before we start.
