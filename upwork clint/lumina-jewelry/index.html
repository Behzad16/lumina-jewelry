Since 2016

Made by hand, six floors above Greene Street

Every Lumina piece is set, polished and finished in our SoHo atelier by a team of nine. Nothing is mass-produced, and nothing leaves the studio until it has passed through the hands of the person who made it.

We work exclusively in solid 18k gold and recycled precious metals, with lab-grown and traceable natural stones. It costs us more. It also means we can tell you exactly where every piece came from.
