1. Who we are

Lumina Jewelry LLC ("Lumina", "we", "us") operates www.luminajewelry.com and sells fine jewelry direct to customers. Our registered address is 148 Greene Street, Studio 4, New York, NY 10012, United States. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, place an order, contact us, or subscribe to our emails.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Order details — name, billing and shipping address, email, phone number, and the contents of your order.
  • Account details — if you create an account, your email address and a hashed password. We never store passwords in readable form.
  • Correspondence — anything you send us via the contact form, email, phone or social media, including photographs you attach to a warranty claim.
  • Marketing preferences — your email address if you subscribe to The Lumina Letter, and whether you have opted out.
  • Reviews — the display name, rating and review text you submit for a product.
  • Personalisation details — engraving text and sizing information you provide for made-to-order pieces.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data — IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and time spent.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see section 4.
  • Local storage — your shopping bag and wishlist are kept in your own browser's local storage so they survive a refresh. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us until you check out.

Information from third parties

Our payment processor confirms whether a payment succeeded and gives us the last four digits and brand of the card. Our shipping carriers give us delivery status. Where you interact with our social media pages, those platforms may provide aggregate analytics.

3. How we use your information

  • To take, process, fulfil and deliver your order, and to handle returns, exchanges, repairs and warranty claims.
  • To communicate with you about an order, an enquiry or a change to our terms.
  • To send marketing emails where you have subscribed — you can unsubscribe from any email in one click.
  • To publish product reviews you have chosen to submit.
  • To detect, investigate and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve it.
  • To comply with our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (orders and delivery), legitimate interests (site security, fraud prevention, improving our products), consent (marketing emails and non-essential cookies), and legal obligation (tax records).

4. Cookies and tracking

We use three categories of cookie:

  • Strictly necessary — keep you signed in, remember your bag, and protect against fraud. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics — help us count visits and understand which pages are useful. These are only set once you consent.
  • Marketing — allow us to measure advertising and show you relevant products elsewhere. These are only set once you consent.

Analytics and marketing cookies are only ever set after you have given consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser. We treat the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal as a valid opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

Your shopping bag and wishlist do not use cookies at all — they are stored in your browser's own local storage and never leave your device until you place an order.

5. When we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only with service providers who need it to do a job for us, under contract, and only for that purpose:

  • Payment processors, to take payment and prevent fraud.
  • Shipping carriers and customs authorities, to deliver your order.
  • Email and helpdesk providers, to send and manage our correspondence with you.
  • Hosting and analytics providers, to run and measure the website.
  • Professional advisers, and law enforcement or regulators where we are legally required to disclose.

If Lumina is ever acquired or merges with another business, personal information may transfer as part of that transaction. We would tell you before your information became subject to a different privacy policy.

6. Payment security

All traffic to and from this site is encrypted with TLS. Card payments are processed entirely by our PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment provider — full card numbers never reach our servers and we cannot see or store them. We do not have the ability to charge your card outside of a transaction you authorise.

7. How long we keep it

  • Order and transaction records — seven years, as required for US tax and accounting purposes.
  • Account details — until you ask us to close the account, then 30 days before deletion.
  • Marketing subscriptions — until you unsubscribe, then we keep a suppression record so we do not email you again.
  • Correspondence — three years from your last contact with us.
  • Analytics data — 14 months in identifiable form.

8. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can ask us to: give you a copy of the information we hold about you; correct anything that is wrong; delete your information; stop using it for marketing; or export it in a portable format.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA). You have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, disclose and share; to delete it; to correct it; to opt out of its sale or sharing; and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell personal information and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information beyond what is needed to fulfil an order. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

EU, UK and Swiss residents (GDPR). In addition to the above you may object to processing based on legitimate interests, restrict processing while a dispute is resolved, and lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any right, email privacy@luminajewelry.com or write to the address in section 12. We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days (CCPA) or one month (GDPR), and will tell you if we need longer.

9. Children's privacy

Our site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

We are based in the United States and our service providers may process your information there. Where we transfer personal information out of the EEA or the UK, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses together with any additional safeguards those transfers require.

11. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our practices change. The date at the top always reflects the current version. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will email subscribers and place a notice on the site before it takes effect.
